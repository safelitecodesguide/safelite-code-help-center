There is no public Safelite price list — the quote is the price. These glass-tier quotes were recorded during checkout testing (2022 models, ZIP 30303, before tax and fees), so you can size up your own quote before you start.

Chip repair pricing

The sample one-chip repair quote was $164.99. With confirmed codes: $139.99 with SAVENOW2026 ($25 off), $144.99 with SAFELITEHEROREPAIR20 ($20 off), $149.99 with WEB15SL or PFF15 ($15 off). The advertised $35 chip-repair deal did not apply automatically in testing — see the troubleshooting page.

Two costs people forget to ask about

  • Camera recalibration (ADAS): after a windshield replacement, recalibration is often necessary; its cost varies by make, model and year, so ask whether your quote includes it before comparing prices.
  • Mobile service: Safelite's mobile option is not unconditionally free — the offer carries a “free*” footnote and a mobile fee can apply. The quote screen shows whether your booking carries one.
Honest limits: “with ADDS100” prices appear only for the two vehicles the code was actually tested on. Other post-code prices are not estimated or invented — run your own quote and apply the code to see your real number.
